## Key Concepts
- Missing values and duplicate detection
- Descriptive statistics, quartiles, variance, and standard deviation
- Correlation and statistical testing
- Distribution plots, box plots, and outlier detection
- Grouping, aggregation, and customer segmentation
- Time-series resampling and rolling averages

## Data Limitations
The e-commerce dataset is a small practice dataset. Its patterns are for learning and demonstration, not for making business predictions. Outliers require investigation and are not automatically errors.
