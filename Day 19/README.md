# Day 19 — Data Science vs. Decision Science

**Research question:** How do Data Science and Decision Science differ in their goals, methods, and roles in business decision-making, and how can they complement each other?

Files:
- `research_proposal.md` — purpose, objectives, hypothesis, scope
- `research_plan.md` — source collection plan
- `comparison_framework.md` — dimensions for comparing the fields
- `analysis_methodology.md` — analysis and quality checks
- `case_study.md` — illustrative marketing example
- `data/marketing_campaign_synthetic.csv` — fictional sample data
- `scripts/campaign_case_study.py` — reproducible demonstration
- `outputs/campaign_comparison.csv` — generated results

Run from this folder: `python scripts/campaign_case_study.py`

The case data is synthetic and illustrative. It does not prove that one field is superior. Cite credible sources in the final research report.
