# Research Plan and Information Collection

## Subquestions
1. What problems does each field typically address?
2. What methods and outputs are common?
3. Where do they overlap?
4. How can they be combined in a business workflow?
5. What are the limitations of comparing them?

## Source strategy
Prioritize peer-reviewed research, academic books or university materials, professional organizations, and reputable technical publications. For each source, record author/organization, title, date, URL or bibliographic details, key claim, relevant comparison dimension, and reliability notes.

## Inclusion criteria
Sources should directly address Data Science, Decision Science, decision analysis, or business use, and provide identifiable authorship or a reputable publisher.

## Exclusion criteria
Exclude unattributed promotional claims, unsupported assertions that one field is always better, and duplicate sources that add no useful information.

## Collection steps
1. Find foundational definitions for both fields.
2. Collect sources on methods, outputs, evaluation, and applications.
3. Record claims faithfully and note differences in definitions.
4. Populate the comparison framework using source evidence.
5. Cite sources in the final report and distinguish source claims from the synthetic example.
