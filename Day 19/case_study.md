# Illustrative Case Study: Marketing Campaign Selection

A company has a limited budget for contacting potential customers. The fictional CSV includes response probabilities, contact costs, and revenue assumptions.

**Prediction perspective:** Who is most likely to respond?

**Decision perspective:** Which feasible group should be contacted to maximize expected net value?

Assumptions: budget ₹300; expected revenue equals response probability multiplied by revenue if the customer responds; contact costs apply to every selected customer; outcomes are uncertain. The script demonstrates a simple greedy selection heuristic.

Compare selected customers, cost, expected responses, expected revenue, and expected net value. This is an educational example, not a real campaign evaluation or proof that one discipline is better.
