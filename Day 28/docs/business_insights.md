# Day 28 – Business Intelligence & Analytics
## Business Insights Report

### 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes a synthetic retail sales dataset using SQL, Python, and Power BI. The objective is to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer activity, product categories, and regional performance.

### 2. Executive Summary

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 182,686.00 |
| Estimated Profit | 79,127.00 |
| Profit Margin | 43.31% |
| Completed Orders | 784 |
| Unique Customers | 149 |
| Average Order Value | 233.02 |
| Top Revenue Category | Home |
| Top Revenue Region | South |

*Note: Currency values are expressed in generic dataset units.*

### 3. Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue performance:** The dataset generated total revenue of 182,686.
2. **Profitability:** Estimated profit is 79,127, representing a profit margin of 43.31%.
3. **Order performance:** There are 784 completed orders in the analyzed dataset.
4. **Customer activity:** The dataset contains 149 unique customers.
5. **Average order value:** Average order value is 233.02.
6. **Category performance:** Home is the highest-revenue product category.
7. **Regional performance:** South is the highest-revenue region.

### 4. Business Recommendations

- Investigate the products and customer preferences driving Home category revenue.
- Study the South region to identify successful sales practices that could be applied elsewhere.
- Monitor profit margins across categories and regions to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Use customer segmentation and repeat-purchase analysis to improve customer retention.
- Track monthly revenue and completed orders to identify sales trends.

### 5. Tools and Technologies

- SQL: Analytical queries and aggregations.
- Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.
- Jupyter Notebook: Exploratory data analysis and reporting.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboard, data model, and DAX measures.

### 6. Data and Interpretation Limitations

- The dataset is synthetically generated for learning and demonstration.
- Results should not be interpreted as actual company performance.
- Currency is represented in generic units.
- Estimated profit is based on the generated dataset and its cost fields.
- Further analysis is required before making real-world business decisions.

### 7. Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates an end-to-end business intelligence workflow, from structured data and analytical SQL queries to Python-based exploration and Power BI visualization. The results identify Home as the leading category and South as the leading region, with an overall estimated profit margin of 43.31%.
