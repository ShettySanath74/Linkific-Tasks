# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Executive snapshot
All amounts are generic currency units (CU) from a synthetic practice dataset.

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total revenue | 182,686.00 CU |
| Estimated profit | 79,127.00 CU |
| Profit margin | 43.31% |
| Completed orders | 784 |
| Unique customers | 149 |
| Average order value | 233.02 CU |
| Top revenue category | Home |
| Top revenue region | South |

## Findings
1. The synthetic dataset records total completed-order revenue of 182,686 CU.
2. Estimated profit is 79,127 CU, equivalent to a 43.31% margin under the simplified cost model.
3. There are 784 completed orders and 149 unique customers in the analyzed dataset.
4. Average order value is 233.02 CU.
5. Home is the highest-revenue category.
6. South is the highest-revenue region.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the products and customer preferences driving Home category revenue.
- Study the South region's sales mix and practices, then compare them with other regions.
- Compare profit margins by product category and region, not revenue alone.
- Add repeat-purchase and customer segmentation analysis.
- Monitor monthly revenue and completed orders to identify trends.

## Interpretation limits
The dataset is synthetic and is not actual company/customer data. Estimated profit excludes shipping, tax, overhead, and refunds. These findings are suitable for demonstrating analytical methods, not for making real commercial decisions.
